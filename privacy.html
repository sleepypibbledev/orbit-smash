Short version: Orbit Smash stores your progress on your device. There are no accounts and we do not sell your data. Ads are shown by Google AdMob, which may process device data as described below.

1. Who we are

Orbit Smash (“the Game”) is published by Sleepy Pibble. Contact: SleepyPibbleDev@gmail.com · Telegram @Fulif_123.

2. Data stored on your device

Progress, coins, upgrades, skins, achievements, settings and best scores are stored locally in the app storage. This data is not sent to us. Clearing app data or uninstalling the Game deletes it. You can also reset progress in Settings.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows rewarded and interstitial ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process the Android advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and ad interaction events to serve and measure ads, and to prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalised ads. You can reset or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Google → Ads). See Google’s advertising policy.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was completed. We never see your payment details.

5. What we do not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no social features.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK of our own.
  • No sale of personal data.

6. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Retention and deletion

Local data stays until you reset it or uninstall the Game. We hold no server-side user data, so there is nothing for us to delete. For questions about data processed by Google, contact Google.

8. Changes

We may update this policy when features change. The effective date above will change accordingly.
