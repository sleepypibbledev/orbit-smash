Help & Support

Need help or want to report a bug? Message me on Telegram @Fulif_123 or email SleepyPibbleDev@gmail.com. Please include your phone model, Android version and a short description of the problem.

Restore purchases

Open Shop → “Restore purchases”. Make sure you are signed in to the same Google account used for the purchase.

Refunds

All purchases are processed by Google Play. Refund requests go through Google Play (Account → Order history).

Reset progress

Settings → Reset progress. This permanently deletes coins, upgrades and skins on this device.
